# Product Vision

## Vision
Build a personal shopping optimization assistant that learns from users' receipts and recommends where future purchases should be made to maximize savings.

North star (from Hugo's proposal): **not a price comparator — a shopping-intelligence platform.** It aggregates prices from many sources, normalizes them with AI into canonical products, and tells the shopper the best *strategy* (best store, best cross-store combination, when to wait). Voiced by **Mia**, the character — the user "opens Mia," not a spreadsheet. See [11_Branding_And_Mia.md](11_Branding_And_Mia.md) and the target architecture in [04_Technical_Architecture.md](04_Technical_Architecture.md).

## Problem
Consumers rarely know which recurring products they consistently overpay for. More broadly, they don't know which store is actually cheapest, juggle several apps, can't price a whole basket, don't know how much they saved, and have no price history. Markets, in turn, have little intelligence to promote deals beyond flyers.

## Value Proposition
Analyze completed purchases and generate personalized recommendations for future savings. Turn thousands of disorganized prices into useful information.

## Target Users
- Families
- Budget-conscious shoppers
- Frequent online shoppers
- Personal finance enthusiasts

## Differentiators
- Receipt-first workflow
- Personalized insights
- Online retailer comparison
- Continuous learning
